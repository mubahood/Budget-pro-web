# Budget Pro: Video Series Plan, A to Z

An introduction video followed by a full training series that covers every screen of the web shop and the phone app.

- **Web:** https://budget-dynamics.schooldynamics.ug
- **Live demo:** /demo
- **Phone app:** Budget Pro on Android

---

## How to use this plan

Each episode has the same parts:

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The menu path and web address (or phone app path) you open on screen |
| **Show** | What to click, step by step, in the order to record it |
| **Say** | The points to mention while showing it |
| **Hook** | The "wow" moment worth slowing down or zooming in on |
| **Watch out** | Traps to avoid on camera, or things to set up before recording |

### Which account to record with

| For | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Most training episodes | **Public demo**: /demo, then "Open the demo shop" (demo@schooldynamics.ug / demo2026, till PIN 1234) | It already has 60 days of trading, photos, debts, suppliers, promotions and a team, in US dollars, which suits an international audience. |
| Sign-up, the setup wizard, team invites, currency change, plan payment, deleting a shop | **A fresh trial shop** made with a throwaway email | These are locked in the public demo on purpose, so it stays usable for everyone. |
| East African shillings, mobile money and M-Pesa/MTN examples | A trial shop set to **Uganda (UGX)** or **Kenya (KES)** | Shows local currency, whole-number prices and mobile money networks. |
| Phone app episodes | The same demo sign-in on an Android phone, or the app's own **"Try the demo shop"** | The phone demo ("Mama Rose Shop") never leaves the phone. It is ideal for practice. |

> The public demo repairs itself every hour and is rebuilt from scratch every 3 days. If a take goes wrong, the next hour puts the settings back. Plan big recording days just after a rebuild (the date is shown on /demo).

### Recording tips
- Record the web at **1440 × 900** and zoom to 125% for small buttons. Record phone screens with the phone's own screen recorder.
- Turn on **Supermarket mode** only in the supermarket episodes (the demo has it on). For "a small shop" episodes, show a trial shop with it off. That shows Budget Pro stays simple until you want more.
- **Hide your notifications.** Use a clean browser profile with no extensions.
- Keep each training video to **3–7 minutes**, with one job per video. Say the menu path out loud: "Sidebar, Shop, Products."
- **Always end with a next step:** "Next video: receiving stock."

# A. Getting started

### A1. Try the live demo first (no sign-up)
- **Where:** home page, "Try the live demo" (or /demo). The same link is on the header "Live demo" and the sign-in page ("Just looking? Open the demo shop").
- **Show:**
  1. The /demo page: what's inside, and the sign-in with copy buttons (email, password, till PIN).
  2. Click "Open the demo shop". You land on the dashboard.
  3. Point at the dashboard strip "Demo shop…" and the pulsing top-bar button "Create your own shop".
  4. Open that button's dialog, then "Keep looking around".
- **Say:**
  - Everyone shares the demo, so don't type anything private.
  - It repairs itself hourly and is rebuilt every 3 days.
  - Messages and payments are switched off in it.
  - The same sign-in works in the phone app.
- **Hook:** one click into a shop with two months of real-looking trading.

### A2. Create your shop (sign up)
- **Where:** /register ("Open my shop free").
- **Show:** Shop name, Your name, Phone or email, **Type of business** (22 types in groups), Password, **Country** ("Shop in Uganda, prices in UGX. Not in Uganda? Change"). Then "Create my shop".
- **Say:**
  - The country sets the currency, the shop's clock and the VAT rate.
  - 14 days free with no card. After that you can stay on the Free plan.
- **Hook:** it's ready in about a minute. The business type decides the starter product list.
- **Watch out:** use a throwaway email. Sign up twice to show a UGX shop and a USD shop.

### A3. The setup wizard (/welcome)
- **Where:** it opens automatically after sign-up. The top-bar pill "3/8" returns you to it.
- **Show** each step: **Your business, then Products, then Money, then Team, then First sale, then Phone app.**
  - **Your business:** icon grid "What do you sell?", country, currency (locked once there are sales), time zone, VAT %, phone, address.
  - **Products:** three tabs.
    - *Start from a template*: tick products and edit prices inline; "Also sell…" mixes in other lists; "Opening stock for all ticked".
    - *Import a file*: CSV or XLSX, "Download a template", then a preview.
    - *Add one by one*.
  - **Money and receipts:** payment methods; mobile money provider and payout number; send receipts by WhatsApp, SMS or Printer; what to do when a product runs out; "cashiers must open a shift".
  - **Team:** up to 3 invites, with "Copy link" and "WhatsApp".
  - **First sale:** "Open the till" (starts the till tour).
  - **Phone app:** QR code and "Open Google Play".
- **Say:**
  - Every step can be skipped and finished later.
  - The phone app resumes the wizard at the same step.
- **Hook:** the template packs come with typical local shelf prices, so you're ready to sell in 3 minutes.

### A4. The getting-started checklist and the till tour
- **Where:** Dashboard, the "Getting started" card; /sell?tour=1.
- **Show:**
  - The 8 items: add products, price every product, first sale, a team member signed in, mobile money, a receipt sent, sign in on the phone, choose a plan.
  - The four coach marks of the till tour.
  - The "First sale done. Well done!" message.
- **Say:** the checklist disappears once you're set up, or you can dismiss it with ×.

### A5. A private demo shop of your own
- **Where:** in the wizard, "Try a demo shop" (header button).
- **Show:**
  1. The demo fills itself with two months of sample trading.
  2. The banner "You're in a demo shop… Deleted after a week".
  3. "Back to my shop" and "Delete demo".
- **Say:**
  - It is separate from your real shop.
  - Your phone app stays in your real shop.
  - Nothing is ever sent from the demo.

### A6. A tour of the screen (the shell)
- **Where:** any page.
- **Show:**
  - **Sidebar:** Dashboard, Chat, Sell, Sales, Promotions / Shop / Money / Settings. "Classic screens" and "Sign out" are at the bottom.
  - **Top bar:** the plan chip, **Search (Ctrl K)**, Chat, the bell, "Sell", and the user menu (My profile, Chat with support, Change password, Sign out).
  - **On a phone-sized window:** the bottom tab bar Home, Sell, Chat, Products, Menu.
  - The list keys `/`, `n`, `j`/`k`, Enter and `?`, and "peek" dialogs.
- **Hook:** in Ctrl K type **"sell 2 rice"**. The till opens with 2 rice already in the cart.
- **Say:** you only see what your role allows, and switched-off features stay hidden.

# B. Selling at the till (web)

### B1. Your first sale
- **Where:** Sidebar, Sell (/sell), or the green "Sell" button in the top bar.
- **Show:**
  1. Search or scan ("Search or scan a barcode…"), then Enter to add. Or tap product tiles (with photos).
  2. Category chips; Tiles or List view.
  3. Cart: − / +, type a quantity, tap the price to change the price, discount or unit.
  4. "Pay {total}" (F4), then "Take payment": Cash, "Exact" and note buttons, change due, "Complete sale" (Ctrl+Enter).
  5. "Sale recorded": the change, "Print (80 mm / 58 mm)", "PDF", "Share", "Send the receipt on WhatsApp", "New sale".
- **Hook:** a full sale with the keyboard only (F2, then Enter, then F4, then Ctrl+Enter).
- **Watch out:** if the shop requires shifts, the till says "Open a shift before you sell". Open one first (see H1).

### B2. Payments: split, mobile money, card, credit
- **Where:** the "Take payment" dialog.
- **Show:**
  - Pick a method, then the amount and a reference.
  - "Split payment" (cash + card).
  - "Credit (pay later)", which needs a customer: "{name} will owe X".
  - Mobile money with its transaction reference.
- **Say:** credit sales go straight into the debt book (Series F), and part-payments are allowed.

### B3. Customers at the till
- **Show:**
  - "Customer (optional)" (F7): search, or "New customer" (Name, Phone), then "Use this customer".
  - With loyalty on: tier (Silver/Gold), points and store credit.
- **Say:** linking a sale to a customer builds their history, statement and loyalty points.

### B4. Discounts, notes, backdating and holds
- **Show:**
  - The "Discount, note, date, location" expander: whole-sale discount, note, sale date (if permitted), sell-from location.
  - Line discount from the cart.
  - "Hold" (F8/F6) and "Held (n)" to restore.
- **Say:** held sales can be resumed on another till when "Held carts" is on.

### B5. The keyboard map: sell like a pro
- **Show:** press F1 or `?`. Then F2 search, F4 pay, F7 customer, F8 hold, F9 no sale, **F10 price check**, + / −, Alt+1…9 favourites, **`3*` then an item** for quantity, `+` to repeat, and Esc.
- **Hook:** F10 price check scans an item without adding it to the sale.

### B6. Printing, the cash drawer, the scale and the customer display
- **Where:** the till settings panel (sliders icon).
- **Show:**
  - "Large text".
  - **Hardware** (Chrome or Edge on a computer): "Connect printer (USB / serial)", paper width, "Test print", "Open drawer"; "Connect scale" with live kg; label printer.
  - Customer display: open /sell/display on a second screen.
- **Watch out:** this needs Chrome or Edge and a USB device. Record it only if you have the hardware.

### B7. Training mode: practise without recording
- **Where:** till settings, "Start training" (managers and owners).
- **Show:** the training banner, a "Training sale" receipt marked "TRAINING — not a sale", then stop training.
- **Say:** perfect for a new cashier's first day. Nothing is saved.

### B8. The offline web till
- **Where:** Settings, Business settings, Supermarket, **"Web till keeps selling offline"**, set to "Always on". It is off by default.
- **Show:**
  1. Unplug the Wi-Fi. The strip reads "Offline — sales are saved on this computer".
  2. Make a cash sale and print the provisional receipt.
  3. Reconnect: "N offline sales waiting", then "Sync now".
- **Say:** offline, the web till takes cash and mobile money at shelf prices only. Promotions and customers come back when online.
- **Hook:** the till keeps selling with the network cable pulled out.

### B9. Supermarket lane extras
- **Where:** the till, with Supermarket mode on (the demo has it on).
- **Show:**
  - **Weighed items:** scan a scale label, or type a PLU such as **4011** for bananas, then "Enter the weight".
  - **Age check** on beer or wine: "Yes, ID checked".
  - **Department keys** (open price).
  - Pack barcodes: a carton scans as a carton.
  - "Take back empties" (deposits), "Sell a gift card", **No sale** with a reason.
  - **Supervisor PIN pad**: a price cut over 10% asks for a PIN (the demo PIN is 1234).
  - Cash rounding and note buttons.
- **Hook:** the supervisor types a PIN on the cashier's screen, and the cashier never signs out.

# C. After the sale

### C1. The Sales list
- **Where:** Sidebar, Sales (/sales).
- **Show:**
  - The "Today" strip.
  - Search "Receipt, customer or phone…"; date range.
  - Chips: All / Not fully paid / Part paid / Not paid / Paid.
  - Filters: Status, Paid by, Sold by.
  - "Export CSV / Excel".
  - Bulk: tick sales, then "Send receipts".
- **Say:** every figure on the dashboard clicks through to this list.

### C2. Inside a sale (the sale peek)
- **Show:**
  - Items, payments, figures (Total, Paid, Still owed, **Profit**).
  - "Take payment", "Print receipt", "Download PDF", "Invoice (PDF)", "Edit customer / notes", "Send receipt".
  - **"Request mobile money"** (the customer approves on their phone).
  - WhatsApp / SMS / Call buttons.

### C3. Returns and exchanges
- **Show:**
  - "Return items": quantity per line, condition ("back on shelf" or not), reason, "Give money back by".
  - **Exchange:** add the new items, and only the difference is paid.
  - "Return without receipt" (supermarket): refund at the lowest price in the last 30 days, with a supervisor PIN, optionally onto a gift card.
- **Say:** returned stock goes back on the shelf only if you say so. Damaged goods don't.

### C4. Voids and reversing a payment
- **Show:** "Void sale" (reason, supervisor PIN); "Reverse" on a single payment.
- **Say:** nothing is ever hard-deleted. Voids leave a clear trail, and stock and cash are put back.

# D. Products and the catalogue

### D1. The Products list
- **Where:** Sidebar, Shop, Products (/products).
- **Show:**
  - The cards: Products, Stock value at cost, at selling price, Running low, Out of stock.
  - Chips: Active, Running low, Out of stock, No buying price, Inactive.
  - Filters and price/stock ranges.
  - **Inline editing:** click a price or reorder level to change it in place. The "On sale" switch.
- **Hook:** changing a price inline, in place, without opening a form.

### D2. Adding a product properly
- **Show:** "New product".
  - **Photo** (drag and drop); name; category and sub-category with quick "New category".
  - Selling and buying price (live margin); SKU (made for you); **Barcode** ("Scan it into this box").
  - Unit; reorder level; opening stock.
  - "Count stock for this product"; "Track batches and expiry dates".
- **Say:** the buying price is what makes profit reports true. The chip "No buying price" finds products that are missing one.

### D3. Packs, units and several barcodes
- **Where:** product form, "Barcodes per pack"; Products, Units (/products/units).
- **Show:** a unit "Carton" made of 24 pcs; a 6-pack and a carton, each with its own barcode and price.
- **Say:** scanning the carton sells one carton, and stock drops by 24.

### D4. Import, export, duplicates and bulk changes
- **Show:**
  - **"Import"**: a spreadsheet, with a preview of price, cost and barcode changes.
  - "Export" to Excel.
  - "Possible duplicates", then "Merge".
  - Bulk bar: "Change prices" by % with rounding and a preview; "Put on sale"; "Deactivate"; "Print labels".
- **Hook:** raise every price in a category by 5%, rounded to nice prices, with a preview.

### D5. The product peek: history, prices and scheduled changes
- **Show:**
  - Figures (in stock, margin, sold in 30 days), "Where it is", "Batches on the shelf", timeline.
  - "Change price": suggested round prices, cost + margin %, **schedule for later**.
  - Price history; prices by level and quantity (supermarket); supplier costs.
- **Hook:** in the demo, the coffee price rise is already scheduled for next Monday.

### D6. Categories, labels and shelf labels
- **Where:** Products, Categories (/products/categories); Print labels; Shelf labels (/products/shelf-labels).
- **Show:**
  - Categories with expected and earned profit.
  - Barcode or QR labels.
  - The shelf-label queue (filled automatically when prices change).
  - Print sheet or ZPL.

# E. Stock

### E1. Stock movements: the stock ledger
- **Where:** Sidebar, Stock, Stock movements (/stock/movements).
- **Show:**
  - Chips: Stock in / out / Sales / Damage & loss / Counts & corrections / Undone.
  - "New movement" with reason and a **photo of the damage**.
  - "Undo" (with a reason).
  - "Export".
- **Say:** every unit in or out has a line here: who, when and why.

### E2. Receive stock (a delivery)
- **Where:** Stock, Receive stock (/stock/receipts).
- **Show:**
  1. "Receive stock": supplier, date, invoice number.
  2. **Scan each item as it comes off the truck.**
  3. An unknown barcode: link it, or create the product on the spot.
  4. Quantity, cost, **batch number and expiry**.
  5. "Paid now" or "Paid in full".
- **Hook:** scanning a delivery in, then creating a missing product mid-scan.

### E3. Landed cost
- **Show:** "Add a delivery cost" (Transport, Duty, Handling, Clearing), spread by value or quantity. The product's cost now includes it.
- **Say:** your margin is finally the real margin.

### E4. Stock counts (stock take)
- **Where:** Stock, Stock counts (/stock/counts).
- **Show:**
  - "Start a stock count": whole shop, one category, or **one aisle** (e.g. A3).
  - The count sheet: scan or type. Filters: Not counted / Differences / Count again.
  - The cards: value of the difference.
  - "Review and post".
- **Say:** sales made during the count stay correct, because the system figure is snapshotted.
- **Hook:** the demo has a "Drinks aisle count" half done. Finish it on camera.

### E5. Transfers, locations and stock requests
- **Where:** Stock, Transfers & locations (/stock/locations).
- **Show:**
  - Locations; "Which phone sells where"; "Move stock between locations".
  - **Stock requests:** "Ask for stock", then approve, then send (truck), then "Receive" by scanning.
- **Watch out:** it needs a plan with several locations (Business or Enterprise). A single-shop account sees the upsell.

### E6. The reorder list
- **Where:** Stock, Reorder list (/stock/reorder).
- **Show:** sells/day, "Lasts N days" (red if shorter than the supplier's lead time), order quantity. Then **"Create N draft orders"**.
- **Hook:** one click turns the list into purchase orders, one per supplier.

### E7. Short-dated stock and markdowns
- **Where:** Stock, Short-dated (/stock/expiring).
- **Show:**
  - Batches expiring and value at risk.
  - "Mark down" 30%, then "Mark down and print label". The label scans at the till at the reduced price.
  - "Return to supplier" or "Write off".
- **Hook:** the demo's yogurt near its date is already marked down.

### E8. Returns to supplier
- **Where:** Stock, Returns to supplier.
- **Show:** "Return goods": lines, reason, refund received. What isn't refunded comes off what you owe.
