# Study of the earlier estate system (kept private)

Estate Ledger replaces a property-management system the owner built earlier for two letting agencies. The full study (its
tables, money rules, screens, reports, how it was really used, its problems and what Estate Ledger does better) lives in the
private portal repo at `budget-pro-new/docs/estate-ledger/00-older-estate-system-study.md`. It describes weaknesses of systems that
are still running and names their clients, so it is not published in this public repo.

What Estate Ledger takes from it, in short:

- the agency model: landlords own houses, houses have units (rooms, flats, shops), tenants rent units, the agency
  collects rent, keeps a commission and pays the landlord the rest;
- commission as a percentage or a flat amount, set on the property and overridable per lease;
- the landlord's monthly report (collections, commission, expenses, payouts, balance) as the agency's main document;
- fees beside rent (garbage, security, water) and the security deposit;
- bills raised a few days before the period starts.

What Estate Ledger does differently is in [03-proposal.md](03-proposal.md) §3 and in the private study's last part.
