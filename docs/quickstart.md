# Budget Pro — quick start for shop owners

1. **Sign up with your phone.** We send a code by WhatsApp or SMS. Add your name, a password and your shop's name.
2. **Set up (about five minutes).** Choose your type of business and country — currency and time zone follow.
   Tick the products you sell from the ready list (or import a CSV from Excel), fix prices and enter what you
   have now. Choose how you get paid (cash, mobile money, credit) and how receipts go out (WhatsApp, printer).
3. **Sell.** Open *Sell*, tap or scan products, take the money, share the receipt. No internet? Keep selling —
   everything syncs when you are back online.
4. **Invite your team.** Team → Invite: a cashier sells, a stock keeper receives and counts stock, an accountant
   sees the money. You decide who gives discounts or sees cost prices.
5. **Keep stock right.** Receive deliveries (or make a purchase order and send it on WhatsApp), count shelves
   with *Stock count*, and check the *Reorder list* when you get the low-stock message.
6. **Know your numbers.** Reports: sales by day, payment and product, profit, what customers owe, VAT. Turn on
   the 8 pm WhatsApp summary under Notifications.
7. **Your plan.** Free for small shops (one phone, 100 products). Upgrade under *Plan & billing* for more phones,
   branches and WhatsApp automation; pay by mobile money or card.

Help: open the menu → Contact us, or reply to any message from us.
