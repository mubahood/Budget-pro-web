# DECISIONS.md — Budget Pro (backend + mobile)

Architecture/product decisions, ADR-style. Newest at the bottom. Format: **Dn. Decision.** Because… /
Consequences. Seeded from `SHOP_ONBOARDING_OFFLINE_MASTER_PLAN.md` Appendix G (G1–G12) and the answers to
its Appendix H open questions (H1–H10). Every later decision made while executing the plan is appended here.

## Seed — Appendix G

- **G1. One sync engine, generalised from the poultry module; the model-cache and `SyncEngine`/`OfflineStore` layers are retired.** Because two competing designs guarantee drift; the uuid/event model fits POS; the poultry engine is proven in production.
- **G2. A server-assigned `server_seq` is the pull cursor; client timestamps are used only for last-writer-wins.** Because device clocks are unreliable and the timestamp cursor loses rows between devices.
- **G3. Stock is event-sourced (`stock_movements`); `current_quantity` is a server-maintained cache.** Because counters can't be merged across devices and the poultry module already proves the derived model.
- **G4. Completed offline sales are never rejected at sync.** Because money changed hands; stock exceptions are surfaced to a human, not refused.
- **G5. Ledger income is posted per payment, not per sale.** Because credit sales are common and the ledger must reflect cash reality.
- **G6. Per-company roles replace the global admin roles for tenants; platform admins keep `*`.** Because every role currently has `*`, which lets any tenant user reach billing/user administration.
- **G7. Phone-first identity with OTP; email optional.** Because the market is phone-first and email deliverability is poor.
- **G8. Legacy mobile routes (`ApiController`, `MobileApiController`) stay until the forced-upgrade window; all new work is additive and versioned under `/api/v1`.** Because a shipped app depends on them.
- **G9. WhatsApp is the primary customer/owner channel; SMS is the fallback; email is tertiary.**
- **G10. The EOQ auto-reorder code is removed; reorder is rebuilt as explainable suggestions on `product_stats`.**
- **G11. Money is `DECIMAL(20,2)` and quantity `DECIMAL(15,3)` server-side; integer minor units on the device.**
- **G12. Demo data lives in a local-only tenant (`company_id = 0`, `is_demo = 1`) that can never sync.**

## Appendix H answers (decided 2026-09-24 so execution never stalls)

- **H1. Branding: the product is "Budget Pro" everywhere** (web admin title, `APP_NAME`, emails, receipts, wizard). The Play Store listing keeps "Budget Dynamics" as the *store name* until P3-10, when it is renamed "Budget Pro" with a subtitle. "InvetoTrack"/"Inveto admin" strings are removed. Because one name is cheaper than three and "Budget Pro" is already the domain and backend name.
- **H2. Pricing: 14-day trial at signup, then automatic fall-back to a new public Free tier** (1 device, 100 products, 200 sales/month, no WhatsApp automation, no multi-branch) instead of a lockout; paid tiers unchanged. Because the informal-retail segment converts from use, not from a paywall, and §2 says never block a cashier. Implemented with quota enforcement in Phase 3 (P3-6/P3-7).
- **H3. Countries: Uganda first, Kenya second, then Tanzania/Rwanda.** Drives MoMo providers (MTN/Airtel → M-Pesa), OTP gateway coverage, tax presets (UG VAT 18%, KE VAT 16%), locales (`en`, `sw`, `lg`).
- **H4. Messaging providers: Africa's Talking for SMS/OTP; Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for WhatsApp; both behind a `MessagingChannel` interface with a log/null driver for tests.** Because AT has native UG/KE coverage and Meta is the only first-party WhatsApp route.
- **H5. Grace policy: 7 days.** During grace, devices keep selling offline and syncing; the web becomes read-only (view/export/billing only). After grace: devices read-only (sync of already-recorded sales still accepted into quarantine and applied on renewal, per Appendix E), web locked except billing.
- **H6. Multi-branch (locations/transfers) and price lists ship in Phase 4 behind the Business plan flag.**
- **H7. The budget/fundraising module moves onto the v2 sync engine in Phase 4**, after the shop; its legacy `MobileApiController` path stays untouched until then.
- **H8. Printing: generic ESC/POS Bluetooth printers first, via a `ReceiptPrinter` abstraction; Sunmi/PAX built-in printers as a second driver later.**
- **H9. Hosting: code is made host-agnostic now** (database queue driver; on the current shared host the worker runs as `queue:work --stop-when-empty` from cron every minute; schedule via cron). Migration to a VPS with a supervised worker is triggered when sync traffic or queue latency (> 60 s) demands it, not before.
- **H10. Stock conflicts can be resolved by `owner`, `manager` and `stock_keeper` roles.**

## Execution log (appended as decisions are made while implementing the plan)

- **E1** (2026-09-24, P0-7) Ledger income is posted per payment. A stand-alone `Sale` stock movement with no `SaleRecord` (legacy quick-sale/mobile paths) still posts its own income row (`source_type = stock_record`) so the ledger stays complete until those paths are retired in §B10.
- **E2** (P0-4) Reversing an *inbound* movement (undoing a receipt after the goods were sold) obeys the product's negative-stock policy like any other outbound movement; reversing an outbound movement always succeeds.
- **E3** (P0-8) Receipt/invoice numbers are assigned when a sale is finalised inside the checkout transaction; a rolled-back checkout may leave a gap in the sequence. Gaps are acceptable, duplicates are not (per-company unique index). The columns are nullable so a draft header can exist for a few milliseconds.
- **E4** (P0-9) Report summaries are cash-basis: revenue = ledger income (payments), expenses = ledger expenses + cost of goods sold of non-voided sales.
- **E5** (P0-12) Forecasting/auto-reorder stay in the codebase behind `saas.features.inventory_automation` (platform flag, default off) rather than being deleted; Phase 4 rebuilds them on the new stock ledger.
- **E6** (P0-14) Subscription checkout is allowed for the company owner only; companies with no `owner_id` on record fall back to users holding the Company Owner/admin role so legacy tenants can still pay.
- **E7** (P0-15) `BillingTest::test_lapsed_tenant_can_reach_checkout_but_not_product` now lapses the tenant by 30 days: a 1-day lapse is inside the 7-day grace decided in H5 and is correctly allowed through.
- **E8** (P0-6) Admin "delete" on a sale voids it and on a stock movement is refused with guidance to reverse; nothing in the shop ledger is hard-deleted, and system-posted ledger rows cannot be edited or deleted (`ledger_locked`).
- **E9** (P0-11) Low-stock threshold: `stock_items.min_stock` per product, else `saas.low_stock_threshold` (env `SAAS_LOW_STOCK_THRESHOLD`, default 10). Default currency for companies without one: `saas.default_currency` (env, default UGX) — never a literal in controllers or views.
- **E10** (P0-18) Poultry tasks, task reports, price history and the audit log have no server tables yet, so they are declared local-only on the phone (never pushed/pulled, never counted as pending) instead of being retried 4× per row every sync. Their backend tables are Phase 4 poultry work.
- **E11** (P0-16) Logout deletes all tenant data on the device (the user is warned when unsynced records exist); signing in to the same company keeps local data, signing in to a different company wipes it first. Data is never merged across tenants.
- **E12** (P0-15) `vendor/` is git-tracked and deployed by `git pull`, so no development dependency may touch it: static-analysis tooling lives in `tools/phpstan` with its own composer.json. Lesson from the f0baeb1 deploy, which briefly took production down until the previous vendor snapshot was restored.
- **E13** (P0-1) The registered account for company 40 now uses a different email than the one it was created with (changed by its user); production verification therefore uses a throwaway tenant that is deleted afterwards, never a customer login.
- **E14** (P1-3) Master-data merge is whole-row LWW with a version check (the "simpler" option in plan A.2); per-field versions are deferred until a real same-row concurrent-edit problem shows up. `changed_fields` is honoured so a device only overwrites the fields it edited.
- **E15** (P1-6) Sync routes sit outside `api.subscription`: pulls always work (devices never lose read access to their own data); pushes are applied during grace and `held` afterwards, then applied on renewal by `SubscriptionFulfillment`.
- **E16** (P1-4) Company `negative_stock_policy` defaults to `flag`: offline oversells are applied and put in the inbox. `allow` suppresses the card; products with `allow_negative_stock` never raise one.
- **E17** (P1-1) Server money stays DECIMAL and is sent as decimal strings; the device converts to minor units locally (plan Appendix A header).
- **E18** (P1-9/B10) The v2 client keeps the legacy local table names and columns (plus sync columns) instead of new table names, so every existing screen and its SQL filters keep working; local `*_id` columns hold LOCAL ids and `*_uuid` columns hold the server reference. The old caches are dropped once and rebuilt by bootstrap (they never held unsynced data).
- **E19** (B10 step 4) Rollout of the offline client is staged through the Play Store percentage rollout rather than a per-tenant `features.shop_v2` switch in the app: the server stays backward compatible (legacy routes untouched), so old and new app versions coexist safely. The `shop_v2` flag is exposed in entitlements for the Phase 2 POS screens.
- **E20** (P1-8) Team management (`admin_users`) and server-computed financial reports stay online features: creating logins needs the server, and reports are generated there. They keep their existing online screens.
- **E21** (P1-8) Poultry keeps its row-level store but now speaks protocol v2 and runs as a participant of the single SyncEngine round; its v1 ms cursors are abandoned (one full re-pull on upgrade, harmless because pulls never overwrite dirty rows).
- **E22** (P1-11) PIN is optional (menu → Security PIN); when set it gates every app start, works offline, and "use my password" goes to login without deleting anything. Five wrong PINs lock for 30 s, doubling each further failure.
- **E23** (P1-10) Photos upload on Wi-Fi by default; mobile data is an explicit opt-in on the Sync & data screen.
- **E24** (P2-3) Credit rules apply to POS/API checkouts that send explicit `payments[]`; the legacy `amount_paid` path (shipped app) and offline sync stay lenient — a completed offline sale is never rejected for credit limits or a closed shift (same principle as stock, Appendix E).
- **E25** (P2-6) A return refunds only money the customer had paid beyond the sale's new net value; otherwise it simply reduces what they owe. If a payment on account landed before the return, the excess is refunded in cash (it shows in that shift's cash-up).
- **E26** (P2-2) Discounts, price overrides, voids and stock corrections are for owner/manager roles (`company`, `admin`, `manager`, `super-treasurer`), cached from `auth/me` for offline use; before roles are known (first launch) the phone assumes an owner so a new shop is never blocked.
- **E27** (P2-5) Receipt text is rendered identically on server and phone (golden files both sides) so a receipt shared offline matches the one shared after sync; thermal printers get the same lines folded to ASCII.
- **E28** (P2-7) Stock-take counts post against the on-hand figure at posting time (not count time), so a sale between counting and posting is not lost; the latest count for a product wins.
- **E29** (P2-1) Poultry's sync wire key for customers is `poultry_customers` (like `poultry_sales`); shop owns `customers`. A test guards against duplicate wire keys.
- **E30** (P3-4) Staff that existed before per-company roles resolve to **manager** (everything except billing, team and settings) so nobody loses what they could do; Ping Pin's `admin`/`member` memberships map to manager/viewer without rewriting their rows.
- **E31** (P3-4) Sync pulls still carry `buying_price` to every phone (the POS needs cost for profit offline); roles without `view_cost` simply never see it in the app or the API.
- **E32** (P3-6) The public Free plan (1 phone, 2 users, 100 products, 200 sales/month, no WhatsApp automation) is also where a lapsed paid plan lands after the 7-day grace, not only trials — the shop keeps selling; toggle with `SAAS_FREE_TIER_FALLBACK`.
- **E33** (P3-6) Proration credits the unused days of a running paid plan at that plan's price in the billing currency; a change fully paid by credit switches at once and turns the leftover into extra days, otherwise the new plan starts a fresh period today. Paying during a trial keeps stacking after the trial.
- **E34** (P3-7) Plan limits refuse only online creations (team members, phones, products); sales are never refused and offline pushes are accepted — usage shows `over: true` instead (A10).
- **E35** (P3-2/P3-8) Companies that existed before the wizard are marked set up and keep every module; new companies get the modules of their business type (all modules when the type is unknown).
- **E36** (P3-3) The demo shop is created on the phone from the bundled retail pack, never on the server; the server-side `ShopDemoSeeder` idea is replaced by the test factories and the template packs.
- **E37** (P3-5) Scheduled notices fire once per shop and period through `scheduled_notices` (a missed scheduler hour only delays them); the daily summary is opt-in, low-stock/unsynced/cash-variance are on by default.
- **E38** (P3-9) Mobile strings live in `lib/l10n/strings.dart` (en/sw/lg, a test keeps keys and placeholders in step); the phone's language follows the shop's locale on first sign-in and can then be changed per phone.
- **E39** (P4-3) The EOQ forecasting/auto-reorder code is deleted (it never ran; production had no rows). Reorder suggestions are max(2 × minimum − on hand, average daily sales × supplier lead time) with the reason shown; the stock-out date is the plan's `forecasting` feature.
- **E40** (P4-5) Per-company uniqueness of master data is enforced on online creates, not in the database: phones create customers, suppliers, categories, units and products offline, and Appendix E keeps both twins and offers a merge (`DuplicateService`, ADR 0007). Server-assigned numbers stay unique in the database.
- **E41** (P4-5) Foreign keys and status CHECKs are added only where the data already satisfies them; `schema:integrity` lists the rest. Statuses are also guarded in the models because MySQL 5.7 parses CHECK without enforcing it.
- **E42** (P4-4) Negative stock is judged on the product total (company policy), not per location; transfers are the exception and refuse to move more than a location holds unless the product allows negative stock. A delivery to a location below zero first covers the shortfall before filling batches.
- **E43** (P4-4) Multi-location needs the plan feature `multi_location` (Business plan, H6); every shop still has a default "Main shop" so movements always have a location.
- **E44** (P4-6) No Sentry SDK or Horizon (vendor/ discipline E12, no Redis on the host): built-in grouped error tracking and a System health page; Sentry is used automatically if the SDK is later installed and `SENTRY_LARAVEL_DSN` set.
- **E45** (P4-6) Deleting a shop is scheduled with the owner's password and a 30-day grace; the nightly purge removes every row of that company only; exports are kept 7 days and never include passwords, tokens or OTP messages.
- **E46** (P4-7) The OpenAPI spec is generated from the routes and the validation rules in the code (FormRequests, CRUD `rules()`, inline `validate()`), not written by hand, so it cannot drift.
- **E47** (P4-8) Legacy routes stay on (rule: "Legacy routes stay live until B10 step 4"). They are counted; when `legacy:status` shows under 5 % of active shops on the old app for 14 days, set `LEGACY_API_ENABLED=false` (old apps see "please update"), then delete `ApiController`/`MobileApiController`/`Utils::get_user` in the following release.
- **E48** (Phase 5) The first Part E items built are the plan's suggested three: WhatsApp receipts (E1), debt reminders (E2), mobile-money request-to-pay (E3). Further Part E items wait for measured demand, as the plan says.
- **E49** (E1) Receipts to customers are business-initiated WhatsApp messages, so they use an approved template (`WHATSAPP_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE`, params shop/number/total/link) with SMS as fallback; the link is an unguessable 32-character token, not the sale id.
- **E50** (E2) Debt reminders are off by default (they speak to the shop's customers); when on, each overdue customer gets at most one per week, at 10:00 shop time, and a manual reminder at most once a day.
- **E51** (E3) Mobile-money collections settle to the shop through a Flutterwave subaccount (the shop's own number), never to the platform account; the payment is recorded only after server-side verification of reference, amount and currency, once, whichever of webhook or status check comes first. Network codes are configurable per currency.
- **E52** (ops, found in production) The shared host limits the account to 25 processes; at busy minutes the scheduler could not spawn a child process per command ("Unable to launch a new process"). Every scheduled task now runs inside the scheduler process (`$schedule->call(Artisan::call(...))`), including the queue worker; a test keeps it that way.

- **E53** (client report, 2026-09-25) Dashboards read sales from one source (`SalesSource`): sale documents that are not voided (net of refunds, profit from the lines) plus the old app's stand-alone Sale movements that were never reversed. Before this, sales from the old app were missing from the overview, the daily chart and profit, and voided sales were counted.
- **E54** (client report) The returns report counts every return, including faulty goods that are not restocked (from `sale_return_items`), plus older stand-alone Return movements. The web sale page records returns through `ReturnService`: good goods go back on the shelf, faulty goods don't. The unused `ProcessReturn` grid action was removed because it wrote `current_quantity` directly.
- **E55** (client report) Write-offs from before Phase 0 kept `quantity_delta = 0`, so they never reduced stock. `stock:apply-old-writeoffs --company=` (dry run unless `--apply`) takes them off the shelf now as new Adjustment Out movements that reference the original row (`reference_type = old_writeoff`). Each takes no more than the stock on hand, and a second run changes nothing. It is run per company and only when the shop asks, because some shops may already have corrected the counts by hand.
- **E56** (web dashboard, 2026-09-25) The dashboard is built on one date range (today, yesterday, 7/30 days, this/last month, this year, custom) in the shop's local days, compared with the same length of time just before. Sales and profit come from `SalesSource` (every app, voids out, returns netted). "Money received" is payments (signed, so refunds and reversals subtract) plus old-app sales and sales from before payment rows existed. "Expenses" are operating expenses only: stock purchases, supplier payments and purchase returns are left out because their cost is already in the profit on sales. Net profit = profit on sales − expenses. Staff see only what their role allows: no profit without `view_profit`, and no money totals without reports, profit or finance permission.
- **E57** Stock counts are applied against the system quantity recorded when each product was counted, not the quantity at posting. This supersedes E28, which erased any sale made between counting and posting (45 counted, 1 sold, posted → 45 instead of 44).
- **E58** Business dates (`sale_date`, `stock_records.date`, ledger dates) are written as the shop's local day (`App\Support\LocalDate`). Rows written before `SalesSource::LOCAL_DATES_SINCE` carry the UTC day, so their local day is read from `created_at` unless the date was set by hand.
- **E59** Shop documents never sit in public storage under guessable names. Receipt, invoice and budget PDFs are streamed to the signed-in user only, and the old public copies were moved to private storage. Financial-report PDFs, which the phone app still opens by URL, get an unguessable file name per report.
- **E60** A sale's status and payments change only through the services: status is read-only on the web, money comes in through "Receive payment" (capped at the balance) and is corrected by reversing a payment. A stock movement that belongs to a document (sale, delivery, return, transfer, count, write-off correction) cannot be reversed on its own, on the web or the API. The stock movement form cannot record sales.
- **E61** Pre-Phase-0 sales posted income per movement (`source_type = stock_record`). Voiding such a sale now reverses that income. A later payment books only the income not already booked. The first time money moves on an old sale again, the amount paid at the till becomes a "Paid at sale" payment row (with no ledger row).
- **E62** Menu headings are found by their contents and never carry a page uri. The shop route allow-list (`AdminAccess::TENANT_PATHS`) is refreshed by migration as well as by the seeders; a test checks that every shop page is on it. laravel-admin's generic `_handle_action_` routes stay closed to shop users, because they build whatever class the request names.
- **E63** (found by the production write smoke test) Voiding a sale reverses every movement on it. A faulty return has no movement, so voiding put the faulty unit back on the shelf. The void now writes those units off again as Damage. Separately, the hidden `company_id` / `created_by_id` / `user_id` fields on web forms are overwritten with the signed-in shop user's own values in `AdminPermission`, so a changed form can't write into another shop.
